## 7. Commission

7.1 **What is paid.** OMS pays you Commission equal to the **Commission Rate (10%) multiplied by the Net Receipts** from each Referred Host for each of that host's **first 12 Paid Months**.

7.2 **When it starts.** Commission starts only when the Referred Host's Free Trial has ended and the host makes their Qualifying Payment. Nothing is paid for trials or for sign-ups that do not become paying customers.

7.3 **Paid Month.** A "**Paid Month**" is a **monthly** billing period of the Referred Host's Latchkey subscription that:
- (a) starts after the host's Free Trial has ended;
- (b) the host has paid for, with Net Receipts above £0; and
- (c) is a regular subscription charge (a new subscription, a renewal, or a change to the plan), and not a separate invoice consisting only of part-period adjustments (see clause 7.6).

A month the host does not pay for is not a Paid Month. A month whose payment is **fully** refunded or lost in a chargeback stops counting as a Paid Month. A month that is only partly refunded still counts, but Commission on it is recalculated under clause 10.2.

7.4 **The 12-month cap.** Commission on each Referred Host is capped at that host's **first 12 Paid Months in total**. This cap is absolute. No further Commission is due on that host after that, however long they stay.

7.5 **When it stops.** If the Referred Host stops paying, cancels or is refunded, Commission stops when they stop. If the host later pays again while this agreement is in force, their remaining Paid Months (up to 12 in total) continue to earn Commission. Clause 20.3 explains what happens after the Stop Date.

7.6 **Adjustments within a month.** If a host changes their plan part-way through a billing period (for example by adding rooms), any charge for that change that is included in a regular monthly invoice forms part of the Net Receipts for that Paid Month. A separate invoice consisting only of part-period adjustments ("prorations") is not a Paid Month and does not earn Commission.

7.7 **Annual plans.** If a Referred Host pays for a year in advance:
- (a) where it is the host's **first** payment after the Free Trial, the annual payment counts as **12 Paid Months**. Commission is 10% of the Net Receipts of the annual payment, recorded as one amount and payable after the Hold Period counted from that payment. No further Commission is due on that host;
- (b) where the host has already had some monthly Paid Months, Commission on the annual payment is 10% of its Net Receipts multiplied by the number of Paid Months remaining under the cap, divided by 12. The cap is then reached; and
- (c) for the Launch Bonus, the first annual payment is the Qualifying Payment, and the "first Paid Month" is the first month of the annual period.

7.8 **Other billing periods and free accounts.** Billing periods other than monthly or annual (for example quarterly) do not earn Commission unless OMS agrees otherwise in writing. Hosts that OMS gives free or complimentary use of Latchkey earn no Commission for that use.

7.9 **Host pricing.** Latchkey's prices, trials and offers are those shown on the Latchkey website at the time, which OMS sets and may change at any time. Commission is always based on what the host actually paid, not on any list price. Nothing in this agreement entitles you to any particular price for hosts.

7.10 **No other payments.** Commission and any Launch Bonus are the only payments due to you. There are no upfront fees, flat fees, retainers, salaries or expenses.

## 10. Refunds, chargebacks, clawback and set-off

10.1 **Full refunds and chargebacks.** If OMS fully refunds a payment from a Referred Host (including under a money-back guarantee), or loses a chargeback or payment dispute on it, the Commission on that payment is cancelled and the month stops counting as a Paid Month.

10.2 **Partial refunds.** If OMS partly refunds a payment, the Commission on it is **recalculated on the Net Receipts OMS keeps**. For example, if a host paid £5.50 and £2.00 is refunded, Commission is 10% of £3.50. The month still counts as a Paid Month. For an annual payment, the Commission is reduced in proportion to the amount refunded.

10.3 **Effect on the Launch Bonus.** Only a refund, chargeback or reversal of the **Qualifying Payment** affects the Launch Bonus. Any refund of the Qualifying Payment, **whether full or partial**, or any chargeback of it that OMS loses, made at any time, **cancels the Launch Bonus in full** for that host. This applies to annual plans too: a partial refund of a first annual payment cancels the Launch Bonus. The Qualifying Payment is not replaced by a later payment. Refunds of later payments do not affect the Launch Bonus.

10.4 **Disputes.** While a chargeback or payment dispute is open on a payment, the Commission and any Launch Bonus relating to it are withheld. If the dispute is decided in OMS's favour, they are released at the next Payout Run (subject to the Hold Period). If it is not, they are cancelled or clawed back under this clause 10. This applies even if the dispute is opened before OMS has recorded the payment.

10.5 **How clawback works.**
- (a) Amounts not yet paid to you are reduced or cancelled.
- (b) Amounts already paid to you are recorded as a **clawback** and **set off** (deducted) against the amounts that become payable to you at later Payout Runs.
- (c) If the clawback has not been fully set off within 90 days, OMS may ask you to repay the balance, and you agree to repay it within 30 days of OMS's request.

10.6 **General right of set-off.** OMS may set off against any amount it owes you under this agreement any amount you owe OMS under it, including clawbacks, overpayments, and losses or costs that you must cover under clause 22.4. OMS will show any set-off on your statement.

10.7 **Overpayments.** If OMS pays you more than is due because of an error, OMS may correct it by set-off under clause 10.6, or by asking you to repay it within 30 days.

## 11. Fraud and genuine referrals

11.1 **What is not allowed.** You must not earn, or try to earn, Commission or a Launch Bonus by:
- (a) **self-referral**: being, or becoming, the Referred Host yourself, directly or through your own business or anyone acting for you or on your behalf;
- (b) **fake hosts**: signing up, or arranging sign-ups for, hosts that are not genuine, independent accommodation businesses;
- (c) **trial cycling**: creating or arranging sign-ups that are kept in trial, cancelled and re-registered, or otherwise cycled to generate Commission or bonuses;
- (d) **false details**: giving false, misleading or incomplete information to OMS or to a host, or using someone else's identity; or
- (e) **someone else's card**: paying, or arranging for anyone to pay, a host's Latchkey charges with a card or account that is not the host's own, or paying a host's charges yourself.

11.2 **OMS's checks.** To detect self-referral and fraud, OMS may compare securely scrambled (hashed) versions of your identity details, email address, phone number, company number and bank or card fingerprints, and hashed IP address and device information, with those of the hosts you refer and of other Latchkey accounts. OMS may also check patterns such as many sign-ups in a short time or a high rate of disputes. Clause 17 and the privacy notice explain this.

11.3 **What OMS may do.** If OMS reasonably believes that clause 11.1 has been breached, or while it investigates a reasonable suspicion of it, OMS may:
- (a) hold any referral, Commission or Launch Bonus for review;
- (b) reject the referral, and withhold or cancel the Commission and any Launch Bonus involved;
- (c) recover amounts already paid under clause 10.5 and clause 10.6;
- (d) suspend your Account and payouts; and
- (e) end this agreement immediately by notice under clause 20.4.

Where OMS's systems find a strong match (for example the same email, phone or card), the referral or bonus may be rejected automatically. OMS will review it if you ask, and may reverse the decision.

## Annex A: Worked examples (illustrations, not a promise)

These examples assume a host paying **£5.50 a month** (excluding VAT), the Commission Rate of 10% and the 45-day Hold Period. Actual host prices are those on the Latchkey website at the time (clause 7.9).

**A1. Monthly host, full 12 Paid Months.**
- Sign-up Date 1 November 2026. Free Trial ends 1 December 2026.
- Qualifying Payment £5.50 on 1 December 2026. Commission £0.55, payable from 15 January 2027 (45 days later).
- If the host pays every month for 12 Paid Months: 12 × £0.55 = **£6.60** in total. Nothing after that.
- Payouts are sent once your payable balance is at least £10 (clause 12.3). With one host like this, your balance would carry forward until it reaches £10, or until other hosts add to it.

**A2. Launch Bonus timing** (if a bonus applies, see Annex B).
- Same host. Sign-up Date 1 November 2026 is on or before the Bonus End Date (condition 8.2(a)).
- The Qualifying Payment on 1 December 2026 is within 60 days after the Sign-up Date, because the deadline is the end of 31 December 2026, UK time (condition 8.2(b)).
- The first Paid Month ends on 1 January 2027 and was not refunded, so the Launch Bonus is earned then (condition 8.2(c)).
- It becomes payable on 15 February 2027 (45 days later) and is paid at the next Payout Run once your balance is at least £10.

**A3. Partial refund.** The host pays £5.50 for month 3 and OMS refunds £2.00. Commission on month 3 is recalculated as 10% of £3.50 = £0.35. If £0.55 had already been paid to you, the £0.20 difference is set off against your next payout (clause 10.5). The month still counts as a Paid Month.

**A4. Refund of the Qualifying Payment.** If any part of the host's Qualifying Payment is refunded, or it is charged back and OMS loses, the Launch Bonus for that host is cancelled in full (clause 10.3). Commission on what the host kept is recalculated under clause 10.2.

**A5. Annual plan from the start.** The host's first payment after the Free Trial is an annual payment of £66. This counts as 12 Paid Months. Commission is 10% × £66 = **£6.60**, in one amount, payable 45 days after the payment.

**A6. Annual plan after monthly payments.** The host pays monthly for 4 Paid Months, then pays £66 for a year. 8 Paid Months remain under the cap, so Commission on the annual payment is 10% × £66 × 8 ÷ 12 = **£4.40**. The cap is then reached.

**A7. After an ordinary stop.** You stop on 1 March 2027. A host whose Qualifying Payment was in December 2026 keeps earning you Commission while they pay every month without a break, up to their 12 Paid Months. A host who signed up in February 2027 but makes their Qualifying Payment on 5 March 2027 earns you nothing (clause 20.3).

**A8. Payout timing.** Amounts that become payable during January 2027 are included in the January Payout Run (if your balance is at least £10), which OMS aims to send by 2 March 2027 (30 days after the end of January).
